## Bug Reports

When reporting a bug / compatibility issue, please include information regarding:

1. Unity version
2. VRChat SDK version
3. Avatar used
4. A list of tools/gimmicks/non-outfit prefabs used on the avatar
5. Steps to reproduce
6. Whether you were in Session or Persist Mode
7. Console errors (if there were any)
8. Screenshot of the InertiaBones window (Optionally a screenshot of the avatar after applying, either in play-mode or in-game depending on where the issue is visible)
9. (If you went through the **Compatibility test**) Please provide information regarding the likely culprit(s).

Providing this information helps me resolve issues quickly.

!!! info
	If you prefer not to fill out a bug report or you aren't sure what to include, then you are free to contact me on X.com or Booth.pm (listed above) and I will do my best to help you.
	Do note that this will likely take more time as I will in most cases need specific information regarding your specific issue after we enter dialog.
